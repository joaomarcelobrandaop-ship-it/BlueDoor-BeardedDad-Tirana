Blue Door Hostel

The name comes from the blue front door. For the owners, it stands for peace and prosperity.

An old house in Tirana, 9 minutes on foot from the New Bazaar and 18 from Skanderbeg Square. It has 38 beds, a garden with an olive tree and an orange tree, a living room with a wood stove and a terrace on the roof.

Every bed comes with free breakfast, a towel and bed linen, Wi-Fi and a locker.

Inside the house

Breakfast is free every morning. In the evening there's a home-cooked Albanian dinner, always with a vegetarian option.

The blue kitchen

Under a skylight of glass blocks. The Saturday cooking class happens here: byrek, tavë dheu, wine and raki.

The living room

A wood stove, yellow sofas and a kilim rug on the floor.

The dining room

A long dark table, cream chairs and orange walls with arched niches.

The garden

Through the gate with the painted sign: an olive tree, an orange tree and a bar in the garden.

Getting here

Rruga Shtjefën Gjeçovi 18, Tirana. On foot, it's 9 minutes to the New Bazaar, 18 to Skanderbeg Square and 21 to The Bearded Dad.

From the airport, take the LUNA bus to the stop behind the Opera. It leaves every hour, on the hour, day and night, costs 400 lek or €4.50 and takes cards. From the stop it's 15 minutes on foot.

Good to know

Is breakfast included?
Yes. Breakfast is free every morning.
Is there a dorm for women only?
Yes. It has 6 beds, in the attic, with a door to a terrace.
Are towels and bed linen included?
Yes, both are free, and so are Wi-Fi and a locker.
Do you have private rooms?
There are two private rooms for one person each. The bathroom is outside the room.
How do I get here from the airport?
Take the LUNA bus to the stop behind the Opera. It leaves every hour, on the hour, day and night, and costs 400 lek or €4.50. From the stop it's 15 minutes on foot.
Can I go to events at The Bearded Dad?
Yes. The Thursday barbecue at The Bearded Dad is for guests of both houses. The two hostels are 21 minutes apart on foot.
Can I bring a pet?
No, pets are not allowed.
Ask us something else
