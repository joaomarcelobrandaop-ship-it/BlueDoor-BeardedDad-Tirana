The Bearded Dad Hostel

HOSCAR 2026: 3rd in Albania

A two-storey villa with a garden, on a quiet street off Zogu I Boulevard, 12 minutes on foot from Skanderbeg Square. The beds are in the main house, in a dorm 20 metres away, in a new villa 100 metres away, in caravans in the yard and in apartments.

Every bed comes with free breakfast, a towel and bed linen, Wi-Fi and a locker with a lock. The city tour is free too.

Dorms in the main house, two newer dorms with their own bathroom, and private places to sleep.

Bunks with curtains, a reading light, air conditioning and a locker for each bed. One of the 8-bed dorms is in a building 20 metres away and shares the kitchen, garden and reception of the main house.

Inside the house

Family Dinner is cooked by someone from the team and served in the garden every night, with one drink and always a vegetarian option.

The garden

Cable-drum tables, pallet benches, tyres painted yellow and string lights. On Thursdays there's a barbecue for guests of both houses.

The hall

Bean bags under a crystal chandelier, with the dorm doors around it. There are board games and a book exchange.

Breakfast

Free every morning, for every guest.

Kitchen and laundry

The guest kitchen has a stove, a fridge and freezer, a kettle, pans and utensils. There's a washing machine and a dryer.

It's a no-shoes house: there are clean slippers every day, or you can bring your own.

Ask to join Family Dinner

Getting here

Rruga Hamid Shijaku, Vila 13, Tirana, off Zogu I Boulevard. On foot, it's 12 minutes to Skanderbeg Square, 17 to the New Bazaar and 21 to the Blue Door.

From the airport, take the LUNA bus to the stop behind the Opera. It leaves every hour, on the hour, day and night, costs 400 lek or €4.50 and takes cards. From the stop it's 13 minutes on foot.

Good to know

Is breakfast included?
Yes. Breakfast is free every morning, and the city tour is free too.
Can I cook?
Yes. The guest kitchen has a stove, a fridge and freezer, a kettle, pans and utensils. The new villa and the apartments have their own kitchens.
Is there a dorm for women only?
Yes. It has 6 beds and its own bathroom, in the new villa 100 metres from the main house.
Do you have private rooms?
Yes: a private room for three with a shared bathroom, caravans in the yard, and apartments for two or three with their own bathroom.
Is there a laundry?
Yes, there is a washing machine and a dryer.
How do I get here from the airport?
Take the LUNA bus to the stop behind the Opera. It leaves every hour, on the hour, day and night, and costs 400 lek or €4.50. From the stop it's 13 minutes on foot.
Do I wear shoes inside?
No, it's a no-shoes house. There are clean slippers every day, or you can bring your own.

Staying at the Blue Door? You're welcome at the Thursday barbecue here.
