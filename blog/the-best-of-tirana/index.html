The best of Tirana: three places to start

30 October 2026 · from a guide by Lost in Albania, the tour company of the hostels

New in Tirana and not sure where to begin? Start with these three. Two of them you can reach on foot from the hostels.

Skanderbeg Square

The centre of the city, named after Gjergj Kastrioti Skanderbeg, the national hero, whose statue stands in the middle. He is the symbol of the resistance against the Ottoman Empire. Around the square are the National History Museum and the Et'hem Bey Mosque. It's also where festivals, concerts and national celebrations happen. On foot, it's 18 minutes from the Blue Door and 12 from The Bearded Dad.

Bunk'Art

During the communist years under Enver Hoxha, Albania built bunkers all over the country. Two of the largest are now museums. Bunk'Art 1, on the edge of the city, was built to shelter the leadership in a nuclear attack. Bunk'Art 2 is near Skanderbeg Square. Both tell the story of daily life and of the political repression of those years, with photos, objects and art.

Mount Dajti

Tirana's balcony: a national park just outside the city, with pine forests, walking trails and restaurants with a view over the valley. The Dajti Ekspres cable car takes about 15 minutes to go up.
