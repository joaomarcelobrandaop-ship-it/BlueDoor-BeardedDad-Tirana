Tirana

In Albanian: Tiranë (tee-RAH-nuh)

The capital of Albania. Mountains on one side, buses all day to the sea at Durrës, and a centre you can walk.

  • 01Skanderbeg SquareThe heart of the city, with the statue of the national hero, the National History Museum and the Et'hem Bey Mosque.Ask us how to get there
  • 02Bunk'ArtCold War bunkers built for the communist leaders, now museums. Bunk'Art 2 is near the square.Ask us how to get there
  • 03Mount DajtiTirana's balcony. The Dajti Ekspres cable car takes about 15 minutes to go up.Ask us how to get there
  • 04The New BazaarFood market, cafés and restaurants, 9 minutes on foot from the Blue Door.Ask us how to get there
