Two houses.

In Albanian: Dy shtëpi (DÜ shtuh-PEE)

Same owners, same WhatsApp. Pick the one that suits you.

Blue Door

An old house 9 minutes on foot from the New Bazaar, with a garden, a wood stove in the living room and a terrace on the roof.

  • Capsule beds, each with a curtain and a locker
  • Mixed dorms of 6 and 8 beds; the 8-bed one has its own bathroom
  • A dorm for women only, in the attic, with a terrace
  • Two private rooms for one person, with the bathroom outside the room

The Bearded Dad

A two-storey villa on a quiet street off Zogu I Boulevard, 12 minutes on foot from Skanderbeg Square. The beds are in a few buildings: the main house, a dorm 20 metres away, a new villa 100 metres away and caravans in the yard.

  • Mixed dorms of 5, 6 and 8 beds; the 5-bed one, in the new villa, has its own bathroom
  • A dorm for women only, with its own bathroom, in the new villa
  • A private room for three, with the bathroom outside the room
  • Private caravans in the yard
  • Apartments for two or three

Bus.

In Albanian: Autobusi (ow-toh-BOO-see)

From the airport, take the LUNA bus. It leaves every hour, on the hour, day and night. It costs 400 lek or €4.50, takes cards, and the ride is 30 to 40 minutes.

Get off at the stop behind the Opera. From there it's 15 minutes on foot to the Blue Door and 13 to The Bearded Dad. An official taxi costs about 2,200 lek.
